# What the reference films do

## vox-director samples (football, Mexican food, money, Silicon Valley, Tang; all 24 fps, about -19 LUFS)
Local copies: `video-gen/vox/vox-director/samples/`.
- A cut every 4.9–5.7 s (measured). Each beat is a wide shot with the headline, then a close detail shot without it.
- A bold flat background colour per beat (mustard, red, teal, navy, pink).
- Dense collage of 15–30 pieces: sticker-bordered cut-out photos, halftone hands holding objects, newspaper scraps, tape, stamps, tickets.
- Confetti layer (triangles, dots, zigzags, squiggles in palette colours), drifting and bursting on key words.
- Big torn-paper headline banner, sometimes ransom-note letters, plus "NEW!" or "TOP SECRET" badge stickers.
- Sunburst rays and glow flashes behind hero objects on payoff beats.
- Motion: hands slide in and out, pieces fly in and settle with overshoot, things scatter, slow camera push.
- Captions: white bold text with a dark outline, 2 lines at the bottom.

## Vox, "The Middle East's cold war, explained" (2017, 10:19, 18M views)
Local copy: `video-gen/vox/vox-director/samples/yt_ref/ref.mp4` (for analysis only).
- 106 cuts in 619 s (about 5.8 s per shot). About -17 LUFS.
- **Map base:** desaturated grey shaded-relief satellite terrain, dark oceans. Countries as semi-transparent textured fills (Iran red, Saudi green, Iraq yellow), relief showing through.
- **Timeline bar:** thin white line with ticks, end years (1900 … 2017), a sliding white ▲, the event in black boxes ("1980 / IRAQ INVADES IRAN"). Persists through the history section.
- **Circle portraits** pinned on countries: the circle colour matches the country, name in white bold sans, role in italic serif. Connector lines to militia icons build network diagrams.
- **Type pairing:** heavy sans caps ("PROXY") + italic serif ("warfare", "Kingdom of Saudi Arabia").
- **Animated thick dotted line** along a front or border.
- **News card:** grey card with outlet logo, headline and date. **Document:** scanned page with a yellow highlight on the key line.
- Archival footage duotone-tinted, with film-burn transitions; expert interview as A-roll (only if real footage exists).
