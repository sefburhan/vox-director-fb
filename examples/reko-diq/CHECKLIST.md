# Reko Diq film: production checklist (v2, "rich" pass)

## A. What the 5 reference films do (studied frame by frame)
Films: football (60s), Mexican food (60s), money (60s, 9:16), Silicon Valley (60s, with host), Tang (30s). All 24 fps, about -19 LUFS.

- [x] **Cut every ~5 s**, steady (measured: 4.9–5.7 s average). Each beat is a wide shot with the headline, then a close detail shot without it.
- [x] **Bold flat background colour per beat** (mustard, red, teal, navy, pink), not one colour for the whole film.
- [x] **Dense collage**, 15–30 pieces per frame: cut-out photos with white sticker borders, halftone hands holding objects, newspaper scraps, tape, stamps, receipts, tickets.
- [x] **Confetti layer** of triangles, dots, zigzags and squiggles in the palette colours, drifting and bursting on key words.
- [x] **Big cut-out headline banner** on torn paper, top or bottom, sometimes ransom-note letters, plus small "NEW!" or "TOP SECRET" badge stickers.
- [x] **Sunburst rays**, starbursts and glow flashes behind hero objects on the payoff beat.
- [x] **Motion**: hands slide in and out, pieces fly in and settle with overshoot, things scatter, slow camera push, a flash on the reveal.
- [x] **Maps**: vintage poster-style world map with colour-blocked countries, a cut-out headline and stickers on it.
- [x] **Captions**: white bold text with a dark outline, 2 lines at the bottom.

## F. Movement and animation vocabulary
- [ ] **Slide-ins from a random side** (left, right, top, bottom) for info sliders and stat cards, with overshoot settle.
- [ ] **Pop and slap**: labels scale 0.55 → 1.05 → 1 with a paper "pop" sound.
- [ ] **Fly-in collage**: posters cut into 3–6 pieces (rembg/BiRefNet) that fly in and assemble.
- [ ] **Stepped 12 fps wobble** on every cut-out (the handmade feel); camera stays smooth at 24 fps.
- [ ] **Camera**: push in, pull out, pan or tilt per shot, varied between neighbours; impact shake on slams; whip-pan with blur between beats.
- [ ] **Angles**: every piece slightly rotated (±2–6°); stat cards tilted; Dutch tilt on the risk beat.
- [ ] **Confetti**: drifting at all times, bursts on key numbers.
- [ ] **Sunburst** rays rotating behind hero numbers ($5.9B, 13.1 Mt).
- [ ] **Hand-drawn** circles, arrows, underlines and highlighter sweeps drawn on stroke by stroke.
- [ ] **Counters** ease out and hold for 0.5 s.
- [ ] **Paper peel** (G1 cutaway), **crumple** (penalty), **stamp slam** (1993, 2011), **gavel** (2013).
- [ ] **AI motion** (LTX image-to-video) for 6–8 hero shots (desert, mine pit, smelter, EV, people), only when the Mac is free of other LTX jobs.

## K. Learned from Vox, "The Middle East's cold war, explained" (2017, 10:19, 18M views)
- [ ] **Map base = grey shaded-relief terrain + dark oceans** (Natural Earth shaded relief, public domain). Countries as semi-transparent tinted overlays so relief shows through. Replaces the flat vector map.
- [ ] Map colours: Pakistan and Kashmir warm cream tint with a yellow border; **Balochistan copper**; Iran teal-grey; Afghanistan muted sand; oceans near-black teal.
- [ ] **Timeline bar, Vox style**: thin white line, tick marks, "1993" … "2026" at the ends, a sliding white ▲ marker, event text in black boxes ("2011 / LICENCE DENIED").
- [ ] **Circle cut-outs** pinned on the map: a photo or icon in a circle with a coloured fill, name in bold sans, role in italic serif (BHP, Tethyan, Barrick, Supreme Court, ICSID, EXIM/IFC/ADB). **Connector lines** show who backs whom.
- [ ] **Type pairing**: bold condensed caps (headlines) + italic serif (country names, roles). Serif: Playfair Display Italic or Libre Caslon Italic (OFL).
- [ ] **Animated dotted borders**: Pakistan border traced in yellow; Kashmir line of control dotted; red dotted pulse along the Iran border in the risk beat.
- [ ] **News cards**: clean grey card with outlet name, real headline, real date (7 verified articles).
- [ ] **Document highlight**: real quote lines from Barrick's 2 Apr 2026 statement and the High Court judgment, swept in yellow highlighter.
- [ ] Archival-style treatment: duotone tint + film-burn/light-leak transitions on real photos.
- [ ] Cut every ~5–6 s; master loudness -14 to -16 LUFS.
- [ ] **No Vox logo or branding** anywhere (style reference only).

## L. Added from the general Vox pre-render checklist (only the gaps)
- [ ] **Arrows** (`engine.draw_arrow`): hand-drawn, drawn on start to end, curved or straight, head follows the curve so it lands at any angle; double-headed for "between"; dashed for routes (ore → port). One stroke width (10 px) per colour role: yellow = point at this, red = danger or blocked, ink = neutral connection.
- [ ] **Marker circles** (`engine.draw_circle_mark`) around the key spot or number, overshooting their start like a real marker.
- [ ] **Red string links** for the saga: BHP → Tethyan → Antofagasta + Barrick → Pakistan, pin to pin, slight sag, drawn on, 4 px, shadow offset.
- [ ] **Type size scale (fixed)**: chapter 120 · heading 72 · callout 46 · labels/map 32 · sources 22 px. Caps tracked +5%.
- [ ] **Reading time**: on-screen text holds ≥ 1 s per 3 words + 0.5 s. Max 6 words per line, 2–3 lines.
- [ ] **Colour meaning, fixed for the whole film**: copper = Reko Diq / copper / Balochistan · yellow = "look here" · red = blocked, penalty, risk, slipping date · teal-grey = neighbours, neutral data · ink = text.
- [ ] **One light direction**: every shadow falls down-right with the same softness (pins, arrows, labels, posters).
- [ ] **Chapter cards** for the 3 acts (THE TREASURE / THE CURSE / THE RACE): same layout, 2–2.5 s.
- [ ] **Date and place stamps** in one format and one corner: "1993 · QUETTA", "JUL 2019 · WASHINGTON".

- [ ] **Charts**: thin axes, direct labels, key series in accent and the rest grey; build axes → data → highlight on the VO.
- [ ] **Document highlight**: dim and blur the rest of the page while the yellow sweep plays.
- [ ] **Motion timing**: in 10 frames, out 7 frames, one easing family; transitions limited to cut, paper wipe and camera push/whip.
