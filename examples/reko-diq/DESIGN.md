# Reko Diq documentary: design and asset plan

Source script: `vox/script/reko diq long.md` (23 min, 8 chapters).
Assets: `projects/reko diq/assets/` (credits in `assets/credits.csv` and `assets/credits.md` for the description).
Outro: The Indus brand kit in `vox/resources/theindus.org/`, appended to both films.

## Rules for both films
- **No mention of AI, models or tools** anywhere on screen or in the credits. The end card lists sources only; full image credits go in the description.
- **One set of headline numbers per film**: the long film and the 1-minute cut use reserves, **15 Mt copper / 26 Moz gold** (Barrick technical report 2025). Never mix with the 3-minute film's 13.1 Mt / 17.9 Moz.
- **Every allegation attributed** (Raisani's claim, critics vs Barrick at equal length, the 2030 date as "reports").
- **Real documents shown as documents**: real page, slight angle, highlighter on the exact line, source tag.
- **News shown as clean headline cards**: outlet, real headline, date, real lead photo. Raw screenshots are kept as evidence and crops.
- **Order**: film → source end card → The Indus outro (built separately, appended later).

## Look (carried over and upgraded)
- Vox-style maps on grey shaded relief; Pakistan per the official map (Kashmir), yellow border; persistent timeline bar 1990–2030.
- Paper-collage texture, one copper accent, bold background per chapter, confetti only on reveals (calmer for the legal chapters).
- Real photos treated as torn-edge cut-outs with tape; duotone on archive photos (navy/cream) so mixed sources look like one film.
- Arrows at varied angles, marker circles, highlighter sweeps, red strings, sliding cards.
- Chapter cards: "CHAPTER 3 · THE REFUSAL", same layout every time, 2.5 s.

## 1-minute cut (Shorts and trailer, 16:9 master plus 9:16 crop)
Draft voice-over, about 150 words, built only from claims in the long script:

> On the twenty-eighth of May, 1998, a mountain in Chagai turned white. Pakistan had tested its first nuclear bombs. Almost nobody was talking about what was already in that ground: fifteen million tonnes of copper, twenty-six million ounces of gold. It's called Reko Diq. In 1993, a caretaker government gave a foreign miner seventy-five percent. In 2011, Balochistan refused the mining lease. In 2013, the Supreme Court voided the whole deal. Then a treaty with Australia sent Pakistan to a World Bank tribunal, and in 2019 the bill came in: nearly six billion dollars. In 2022, Pakistan traded the penalty for a new partnership, and wrote a law to protect it. The world needs copper, and the lenders lined up. Then, in 2026, the operator slowed down, and the date slipped to 2030. Thirty years on, the copper produced is zero.

Shots (about 4 s each): Ras Koh archive → map push into Chagai → cutaway with counters → 1993 contract + 75/25 bar → 2011 red stamp → Supreme Court photo + VOID → treaty cover → $5.9B slam + award headline → 2022 signing → copper montage + lender stack → Barrick 2 Apr 2026 statement highlight → 2028 → 2030? → counter resets to 0 → REKO DIQ title.
